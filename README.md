## How I like to learn

```text
Question → Notebook → Experiment → Something breaks
    ↑                                  ↓
    └──────── Insight ← Debugging ←───┘
                     ↓
              A small useful project
```

The goal isn't only to get an output. It's to understand what changed, what surprised me, and what I would try next. When I publish a notebook, I want it to tell the story of the experiment—not just show a result.
