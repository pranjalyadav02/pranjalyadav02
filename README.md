## The person behind the commits

Hey, I'm **Pranjal**. Right now, most of my technical curiosity is focused on **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning with Python**.

I like to understand how things work, not only make them run. **Jupyter Notebook is my experiment desk**: a place to explore ideas, work with data, test assumptions, make mistakes, and keep track of what I learn. I'm still learning, and I'd rather show that journey honestly than pretend I've already reached the finish line.

## My progress doesn't happen in one place

| 💻 At the keyboard | 🌿 Away from the keyboard |
| --- | --- |
| Learning Python and AI/ML concepts | Thinking through problems before opening the laptop |
| Experimenting in Jupyter Notebook | Working on ideas or projects offline |
| Debugging, testing, and building | Spending time with family and friends |
| Turning useful ideas into projects | Enjoying life, recharging, and making memories |

> **My commit graph shows when I push, not every time I make progress.** Some days end with a commit. Others end with a solved problem, a notebook full of experiments, a plan for the next session, or a really good day with my people. That counts, too.
